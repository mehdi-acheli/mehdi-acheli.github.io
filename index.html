01 About

I am an AI researcher with a PhD in computer science, driven by research and innovation in data science.

My doctoral work at Université Paris-Dauphine-PSL introduced me to process science. I developed methods to discover behavioral patterns in the event logs of flexible processes, where traditional process models fail to capture how work actually unfolds. I then moved to industry R&D, first predicting process behavior at Logpickr, then at Sogeti, part of Capgemini, where I led a research project on fraud detection: identifying anomalies in financial processes from historical event-based data.

Returning to academia as a postdoctoral researcher at Télécom SudParis, where I supervised PhD students and interns, I turned to the Cloud-Edge-IoT continuum. There, too, processes are central: a service composition is itself a process, coordinating services with dependencies and execution order. My work followed this process through its lifecycle across cloud, edge and IoT devices: generating compositions from user intent with large language models, monitoring them for anomalies as they run, and using language models again to renegotiate service-level agreements when anomalies occur. Monitoring the continuum also means running learning models close to the devices themselves, which led me to work on training models on resource-constrained devices at its lower layers. Beyond the continuum, I worked with a cybersecurity firm on predicting security ratings from companies’ rating histories, another form of event-based historical data, and drew on my experience with language models to automate third-party risk assessment questionnaires.

Today, as a postdoctoral researcher at CEA, I focus on adapting learning models to the edge and IoT. This line of work, which began at Télécom SudParis with lightweight anomaly detection through knowledge distillation and a dual-model training approach, now continues with my research on federated learning across devices with heterogeneous resources.

02 Research interests

Federated & edge learning

Training and optimisation of models in resource-constrained environments (Edge, IoT, embedded); federated learning under resource heterogeneity; knowledge distillation.

Cloud-Edge-IoT continuum

Anomaly detection, orchestration process, its monitoring and adjustment, and agentic AI for the Cloud-Edge-IoT continuum.

Process science

Mining event logs, behavioural pattern extraction, causal inference, and next-activity prediction inspired by NLP and computer vision.

Graph learning & AutoML

Graph neural networks for fraud detection and predictive process monitoring, data imbalance, synthetic data generation, and reinforcement-learning-based neural architecture search (RL-GraphNAS).

Security rating

Security rating systems and supplier risk assessment.
